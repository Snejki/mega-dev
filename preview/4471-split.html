Original problems and separate fixes

  1. Month-first shared parser for day-first tenants (parser). The shared parser reads dates as MM/DD even when the tenant writes DD/MM. This is the main cause behind #4471. Repair the parser's handling of the tenant's date order.
  2. Misleading date hint in booking-v3 (hint). For day-first tenants, booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. People enter the date month-first as instructed, but the form interprets it in the tenant's day-first order. Correct the displayed hint to match the date order that the form actually reads. This needs its own ticket and fix.
